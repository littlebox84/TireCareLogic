# Brownie's Tire Service Public Catalog Research

Brownie's public tire pages are now treated as a first-party shop catalog observation source, separate from actual physical inventory.

## Public passenger brands observed

- Goodyear
- Dunlop
- Kelly

The Brownie's brand pages expose large model catalogs. Examples observed on the current Goodyear page include Assurance Fuel Max, WinterCommand Ultra, Integrity, Assurance All-Season, Assurance MaxLife 2, Eagle RS-A, Eagle F1 families, Eagle Sport All-Season, Assurance ComfortDrive, Assurance WeatherReady 2, Assurance RangeMax, Wrangler Territory HT, Wrangler Fortitude HT, Wrangler Workhorse AT / AT 2, Wrangler Steadfast HT, Wrangler SR-A and many additional OE/performance/winter products.

The current Dunlop page likewise exposes a large product catalog including SP Sport Maxx families, Grandtrek families, Conquest Touring, Sport Maxx Race 2, SP Sport 600, SP Sport 2030, SP Sport 5000 variants and many legacy/OE products.

The current Kelly page includes Edge Touring Plus, Winter Access, Edge Touring A/S, Edge Sport, Safari AT and Safari MT.

## Current offline snapshot

The offline TireCare catalog stores exact captured size lists for selected Brownie's model pages and keeps the Brownie's brand/model source registry available for further imports. Brownie's public product listing is not presented as on-hand inventory.

## Inventory distinction

Three concepts remain separate:

1. `Brownie's public catalog` — products displayed by the public site.
2. `manufacturer technical data` — load, speed, rim, pressure and product specifications.
3. `shop physical inventory` — what is actually in the Glenwood building, populated from physical labels/photos plus register inventory.

This prevents a public catalog listing from being mislabeled as an item physically in stock.
