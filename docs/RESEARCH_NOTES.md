# Research Notes

Starter sources used by TubeLogic v0.1:

## Bridgestone Americas Tube Business

Light Truck — Made in USA:
https://firestonetubes.com/products/page/19/light-truck-made-in-usa

Light Truck — Imported:
https://firestonetubes.com/products/page/2/light-truck-imported

Valve table:
https://firestonetubes.com/valves/home/

Key starter finding:
`7.00R/7.50R16LT` is not represented by one universal valve configuration. Current Bridgestone listings include multiple articles with TR15CW, TR13, TR135, TR150, and TR440 valves, including different valve offsets and a center-valve configuration.

## Michelin

Agricultural rim/tube/valve reference:
https://business.michelinman.com/help-advice/farm-vehicles/tractor-tire-parts-rims-inner-tubes-valves

Key starter valve details:
- TR13: ~11.5 mm valve-hole diameter
- TR15: ~16 mm valve-hole diameter
- TR218A: ~15.7 mm valve-hole diameter, air/water valve

## Research rule

Never promote a relationship to `verified` unless a credible technical or manufacturer source directly supports it.
